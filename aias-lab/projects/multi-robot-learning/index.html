Multi-Robot Learning & Coordination

Distributed learning and control for networked robots that must coordinate, adapt, and remain safe as a team.

AIAS LabPurdue University Northwest

Research Focus

  • Graph neural networks
  • Distributed reinforcement learning
  • Formation control
  • Cooperative manipulation
  • Scalable coordination

Research Direction

This research studies distributed learning and control for teams of autonomous robots that must coordinate under communication limits, uncertainty, and changing tasks. Graph-based representations and decentralized decision-making are used to capture interactions among agents.

Methods and Applications

Topics include graph neural networks, distributed reinforcement learning, formation control, cooperative manipulation, and scalable coordination for heterogeneous robot teams.
