Irfan Ahmad Ganie

I am an Assistant Professor of Electrical Engineering at Purdue University Northwest, where I direct the Adaptive Intelligence and Autonomous Systems (AIAS) Lab.

My research develops adaptive and learning-based control methods for intelligent autonomous systems, with emphasis on safe reinforcement learning, neural control, human–robot collaboration, cooperative robotics, and cyber-physical systems.

Before joining Purdue University Northwest, I was an Assistant Professor of Electrical Engineering at Wilkes University and a Research Associate at Missouri University of Science and Technology.

Research

Our goal is to develop adaptive, safe, and intelligent control frameworks that allow autonomous systems to learn online, cooperate with humans and other robots, and remain reliable under uncertainty and physical constraints.

Adaptive Learning Control

  • Adaptive optimal control
  • Online reinforcement learning
  • Continual and lifelong learning
  • Safety-critical nonlinear control

Intelligent Robotics

  • Human–robot collaboration
  • Cooperative multi-robot control
  • Robot manipulation
  • Autonomous mobile systems

Neural & Cyber-Physical Systems

  • Spiking neural networks
  • Graph neural control
  • Energy-efficient learning
  • Cyber-physical safety
