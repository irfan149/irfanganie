Irfan Ahmad Ganie

I am an Assistant Professor of Electrical Engineering at Purdue University Northwest, where I direct the Adaptive Intelligence & Autonomous Systems (AIAS) Lab.

My research develops adaptive and learning-based control methods for intelligent autonomous systems, with emphasis on safe reinforcement learning, neural control, human–robot collaboration, cooperative robotics, humanoid robotics, and cyber-physical systems.

Research

Our goal is to develop adaptive, safe, and intelligent control frameworks that allow autonomous systems to learn online, cooperate with humans and other robots, and remain reliable under uncertainty and physical constraints.

Adaptive Learning Control

  • Adaptive optimal control
  • Learning-based control
  • Online reinforcement learning
  • Continual and lifelong learning
  • Safety-critical nonlinear control

Intelligent & Humanoid Robotics

  • Humanoid robotics
  • Human–robot collaboration
  • Cooperative multi-robot control

  • Autonomous mobile systems
